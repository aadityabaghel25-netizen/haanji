# The problem, in numbers (with sources)

Collected 9 Nov 2026. Many figures in this space come from vendors with something to sell, or from outside India.
Each line says which, so we only quote what holds up.

## 1. Customers want to call, and many calls go unanswered
| Figure | Source | How solid |
|---|---|---|
| **76%** of Indian consumers favour business calls; **79%** of businesses say customers skip calls from unknown numbers | Truecaller × Tata Tele Business Services report ([The Wire/PTI](https://m.thewire.in/article/ptiprnews/79-percent-indians-avoid-unknown-business-calls-reveals-truecaller-and-tata-tele-business-services-report/amp)) | India, recent, company report |
| **28%–62%** of calls to businesses go unanswered (CallRail 28%; Invoca, 70M calls, 44%; 411 Locals 2016, 85 small businesses, 62%) | [summary](https://getaira.io/blog/missed-business-calls-statistics), [Alliance](https://www.alliancevirtualoffices.com/virtual-office-blog/shocking-research-finds-small-businesses-miss-almost-half-of-incoming-calls/) | Mostly US, vendor data; quote as a range |

## 2. Most of those calls are the same question
| Figure | Source | How solid |
|---|---|---|
| "Where is my order?" is **~20%–50%** of e-commerce support contacts; DigitalGenius measured **21%** across its customers (Mar 2023) | [DigitalGenius](https://www.digitalgenius.com/blog/what-is-a-normal-wismo-rate), [Macha](https://www.getmacha.com/blog/ecommerce-customer-service-statistics), [Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/wismo-in-ecommerce/) | Vendor estimates; 21% is measured |

## 3. Indian small sellers carry delivery risk
| Figure | Source | How solid |
|---|---|---|
| Cash on delivery is **~60%–65%** of Indian e-commerce orders; Meesho prepaid was **24.9%** of shipped orders (Q1 FY26) | [Upstox, citing ET Prime and Meesho filings](https://upstox.com/news/upstox-originals/investing/is-cash-on-delivery-quietly-costing-you-more/article-184260) | India; Meesho figure from filings |
| **~26%** of COD orders return to origin (undelivered), vs under 2% prepaid (Shipway FY25) | [Shipway ShipNotes via Cargo Breaking News](https://cargobreakingnews.com/?p=34465), [Shipmozo](https://www.shipmozo.com/blog/ecommerce-returns-and-reverse-logistics) | India, logistics company data |
| Small sellers are **85%** of India's social-commerce players; up to **40 million** could sell this way | [Bain × Sequoia via Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/social-commerce-in-india-poised-to-hit-16-bn-gmv-by-2025) | Older forecast; quote as "Bain-Sequoia" |

## 4. "Mila hi nahi" turns into chargebacks
| Figure | Source | How solid |
|---|---|---|
| In apparel, **"not received" is 37%** of chargebacks; **79%** of chargebacks are friendly fraud (customer, not a criminal) | [Chargeflow report](https://www.jotup.co/node/2722828) | Global, vendor data |
| Merchants estimate friendly fraud at **~44%** of chargebacks | [Chargebacks911 field report](https://chargebacks911.com/chargeback-field-report/) | Global merchant survey |

## 5. Callers mix Hindi and English, and menus frustrate them
| Figure | Source | How solid |
|---|---|---|
| Hindi speakers put **12%–13%** English words into "Hindi-only" speech | [arXiv 1406.4824](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1406.4824) | Academic, small experiment |
| **69%** say IVR menus make it hard to describe a problem; only **3%** like using them | [eWeek](https://www.eweek.com/small-business/consumers-dissatisfied-with-interactive-voice-response/) | US survey |

## What we don't have
- No public figure for how many support calls an Indian small seller gets per day, or in which language. The honest
  next step is to ask 10–20 Instagram/D2C sellers, and to pilot on one shop's real (consented) calls.
- No India-wide share of chargebacks that are "item not received". The 37% apparel figure is global.
